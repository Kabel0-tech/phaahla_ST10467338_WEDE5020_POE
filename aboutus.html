The YEAST Journey

From Just a Dream To Your Family Table

Founded in early 2023 by Estelle, YEAST Artisan Bakery & Grocer started as an intimate busuness with a mission to be known for the best quality artisan baking in the local community we serve and for fresh, best quality produce in our independent grocer. We operate across two settings in Gauteng: Bedford Arcade in Bedfordview and Dunvegan in Edenvale. We take pride in being a warm neighbourhood nucleus where quality, service, and care come first. YEAST is built around the joy of wholesome and honest food.
